3. Public distribution — proposed first release

Working recommendation: 250,000,000 ZETHO initially available, equal to 10% of the 2.5 billion public allocation.

  • Initial public release: 250 million ZETHO.
  • Remaining public allocation: 2.25 billion ZETHO reserved for later distribution under a schedule and published rules that are still to be defined.
  • Initial distribution should use transparent per-wallet limits and participation requirements to reduce concentration by a small number of large buyers.
  • Wallet limits cannot guarantee one allocation per human being because individuals can control multiple wallets. Stronger identity checks would add cost, privacy considerations and friction.
  • These initial distribution limits do not control open secondary-market trading after listing.

The first-release quantity and later public-release schedule remain subject to final approval and a clear distribution mechanism.

4. Founder vesting and exceptional allowance

Working founder allocation: 1.5 billion ZETHO.

  • Ordinary vesting proposal: 1.35 billion ZETHO (90% of the founder allocation) released linearly over 36 months, equivalent to 37.5 million per month, subject to final start date and technical implementation.
  • Exceptional allowance: up to 150 million ZETHO (10% of the founder allocation), one-time cumulative maximum, not an automatic release or automatic sale.
  • Exceptional release conditions must be objectively defined before launch. A sudden price spike alone, or a sharp crash, should not qualify. Proposed considerations include sustained genuine demand, adequate liquidity, price stability over a defined observation period and safeguards against manipulation.
  • If the exceptional conditions are never met, the 150 million remains locked under the current recommendation; it does not automatically unlock at the end of the 36-month vesting period.

The observation period, measurable thresholds, independent data sources, approval process, technical lock and treatment of any unused balance still need to be finalised.

5. Initial liquidity — budget and control

  • Liquidity allocation: 250 million ZETHO in total, reserved; this does not mean all 250 million will be deposited into the first pool.
  • Proposed trading pair: ZETHO/USDC, subject to final pool design and platform availability.
  • Creator's stated risk budget for possible initial liquidity: R1,000–R3,000 maximum. This is a budget ceiling for planning, not a commitment to spend it.
  • Initial pool size, exact ZETHO/USDC amounts, LP-token control, lock/withdrawal policy and public disclosures are not yet decided.

A very small pool can be created with a small seed amount, but it will be shallow: relatively modest trades can move the price sharply and sellers may face substantial slippage. The proposed US$0.00001 price cannot be guaranteed by the project and should not be presented as a reliable market price.

Before any mainnet pool is created, the project must publish who controls the liquidity position, whether and how it can be withdrawn, what any lock actually covers, and the risks to liquidity providers and traders.

6. Testing before mainnet

  1. Use Solana Devnet and free test SOL to test mint creation, decimals, supply and token-account setup. Devnet tokens have no monetary value.
  2. Test transfers to the intended allocation wallets and reconcile the total supply against the allocation table.
  3. Verify mint authority and freeze authority settings before any mainnet creation decision.
  4. Rehearse the liquidity workflow and verify the current pool program, fees, account rent and wallet prompts. Do not assume a third-party DEX's mainnet pool-creation flow is available or identical on Devnet.
  5. Only after test results are reviewed and all decisions are approved should mainnet deployment and real spending be considered.

Devnet testing is intended to use free airdropped test SOL; faucet limits may apply. Never send real SOL or other real assets to a Devnet-only wallet.

7. Decisions still open before launch

  • Final approval of the 250 million initial public release and schedule for the remaining 2.25 billion.
  • Exact founder vesting start date, implementation and exceptional-release criteria.
  • Whether unused exceptional founder tokens remain locked permanently or can only be changed by a disclosed formal amendment.
  • Final initial liquidity amount, pool type, seed ratio and LP custody/lock arrangements within the R1,000–R3,000 risk ceiling.
  • Final token authority configuration, metadata, wallet allocation map, launch process and applicable legal/tax review.
