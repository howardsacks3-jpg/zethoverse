DEVELOPMENT PLAN

THE ROADMAP

Thoughtful steps. Built on solid foundations.

ZETHOVERSE is being developed towards a coordinated public reveal. The stages below describe the intended sequence; progression depends on readiness, resources and review.

The objective is to present a complete and credible project rather than release disconnected pieces of an unfinished design.

STAGE 01 · FOUNDATION

Project identity and concept

Establish the ZETHOVERSE identity, website, creative direction, core idea and initial research.

Focus: project story, website, litepaper and development principles.

STAGE 02 · COIN IDENTITY

Artwork, name and visual design

Complete the coin artwork and decide the final coin name and ticker. Bring the coin identity and the wider ZETHOVERSE visual language together.

The final coin image and name will be published only once approved.

STAGE 03 · TOKENOMICS AND ARCHITECTURE

Define the actual proposition

Determine supply, allocation, any vesting or treasury arrangements, intended functionality, production blockchain and launch structure. Document the decisions clearly before contract implementation.

No tokenomics or production network is currently approved as final.

STAGE 04 · TECHNICAL PREPARATION

Build, test and review

Prepare the production smart contract, test intended behaviour, review access controls and dependencies, check the compiled source and assess security. Estimate deployment, liquidity and operational costs before any commitment.

A generic, unbranded Base Sepolia test was used to verify the basic deployment workflow. It is not the official ZETHOVERSE coin.

STAGE 05 · COORDINATED PUBLIC REVEAL

Present the complete project together

Prepare the website, approved coin artwork, final identity, tokenomics, technical documentation and official contract information for a coordinated public presentation.

The website will identify the authoritative channels and contract details when these are approved.

STAGE 06 · PUBLIC LAUNCH AND REVIEW

Observe, learn and decide what follows

If technical, operational and applicable legal requirements have been addressed, proceed with the approved launch arrangements. Monitor genuine public engagement, questions, market activity and risks before deciding on further development.

A launch does not guarantee demand, liquidity, listings or token value.

Important: This roadmap is an intended sequence, not a promise of dates, market performance or outcomes. No official ZETHOVERSE coin is currently available to buy. Final identity, tokenomics, network and launch details will be announced only when approved.
