THE ROADMAP

Thoughtful steps. Built on solid foundations.

ZETHOVERSE will develop through research, careful decisions and practical testing. The stages below describe a possible direction, not a promise of delivery.

The project is at an early concept stage. Progression from one stage to the next will depend on feasibility, resources, security, appropriate guidance and a clear reason to proceed.

STAGE 01 · CURRENT FOUNDATION

Identity and concept

Establish the ZETHOVERSE identity, explain the central idea, document guiding principles and create a clear public information point.

Focus: website, About page, Litepaper and project documentation.

STAGE 02 · RESEARCH

Feasibility and direction

Research possible technical architectures, blockchain options and potential use cases. Compare practical costs, limitations, security considerations and operational requirements.

No blockchain selection, token design or technical commitment has been finalised.

STAGE 03 · VALIDATION

Small-scale prototype exploration

If research supports it, consider a limited, low-cost prototype to test whether a specific part of the concept can work in practice.

Any prototype would require a defined purpose, budget approval and security review before work begins.

STAGE 04 · FEEDBACK AND COLLABORATION

Community and partnerships

Invite constructive feedback and explore suitable technical, creative and professional collaboration when the project is ready.

No partners, sponsors, community programme or commercial arrangements are currently confirmed.

STAGE 05 · FUTURE REVIEW

Decide whether to proceed

Review what has been learned and determine whether further development is justified. Any future product, ecosystem or token decision would be considered separately and only after appropriate technical, legal, compliance and security assessment.

A token launch is not a promised roadmap outcome.

Important: This roadmap is indicative and may change. No dates, deliverables, partnerships, token launch, listing, functionality or financial outcomes are guaranteed. ZETHO remains a proposed name and ticker concept only; no token has been launched or offered for sale. Supply, allocation, utility and blockchain remain under review.
