Unexpected authority change, unauthorised mint, vesting permission change, reserve-cap breach or suspicious treasury movement.

Planned immediate email

Planned incident email

Planned digest
05 / Build sequence

From observatory design to live protection

  1. Finish the evidence baseline
    Confirm the relevant program IDs, authorities, multisig configuration and source/audit correspondence.
  2. Build a read-only Devnet monitor
    Use public RPC and test fixtures; no wallet keys, signing or transaction permissions.
  3. Test alert logic
    Simulate authority changes, unexpected releases, missed checks and reserve-cap violations.
  4. Connect email delivery
    Verify recipient, severity routing, delivery and duplicate-alert controls before enabling notifications.
  5. Enable Mainnet observation
    Only after the final ZETHO mint, vesting program, vaults and governance addresses are independently confirmed.
Safety boundary: ZETHOSPHERE will observe and report. It will never request a seed phrase, private key or wallet signature, and it will not create streams, change permissions, transfer tokens or attempt to “fix” an alert automatically.
