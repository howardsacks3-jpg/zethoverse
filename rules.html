PROJECT RULES

The operating boundaries and token-allocation principles ZETHOVERSE intends to follow. Final technical schedules and mechanisms must be published before any corresponding release or distribution begins.

1. Project role and operating boundaries

ZETHOVERSE's role is to develop the project, issue ZETHO, publish its tokenomics and allocation rules, and administer its own token holdings and releases in accordance with the commitments published by the project.

ZETHOVERSE does not operate a public subscription desk, accept customer applications or subscription orders, solicit or process customer purchase orders, match buyers with sellers, arrange or execute transactions on behalf of buyers or sellers, or charge brokerage or transaction fees. It does not provide investment advice or act as an intermediary for users.

The project does not accept customer deposits, hold customer funds or crypto assets, safeguard users' private keys, control users' wallets, or execute trades for customers. Users deal independently with their own wallets, blockchain networks and any independent third-party exchanges or platforms that choose to support ZETHO.

Those independent platforms are responsible for their own listing decisions and for the exchange, order-matching, custody, deposit, withdrawal and transaction services they provide. ZETHOVERSE does not control whether a third party lists ZETHO or how users trade it after listing.

The creator may retain, release, sell or acquire ZETHO for the creator's own account, subject to applicable law and the project's published allocation, vesting and disclosure commitments. The creator does not transact in crypto assets on behalf of customers or manage customer assets.

2. Allocation and vesting commitments

Any allocation, vesting schedule, release date, restriction or permitted exception becomes a project commitment when it is formally published as final. Draft figures or proposals are not final commitments until clearly identified and published as such.

Published allocation and vesting commitments must be honoured. Releases must be administered in a timely, reliable and consistent manner, in accordance with the published schedule and its stated conditions.

Negligent failure by the creator to administer a published allocation or vesting commitment properly is a breach of these project rules. No undisclosed release, diversion, reallocation or change to an allocation is permitted.

Any permitted exception, amendment or delay must be disclosed publicly, with an explanation, as soon as reasonably practicable. The project must take reasonable steps to correct an administrative error or missed action and explain the corrective steps publicly.

3. Exceptional circumstances and delays

A delay may be necessary where a genuine technical, security, legal or network issue prevents a safe or lawful release. Such a delay is not permission to disregard the schedule. The reason, affected allocation, expected next steps and any revised timing must be communicated publicly and updated when material information changes.

Any exception must fall within the conditions and limits expressly stated in the final published tokenomics. An exception must not be invented or applied retrospectively to excuse an undisclosed or unauthorised release.

4. Transparency and changes

Material changes to token supply, allocation, vesting, release conditions or project administration must be disclosed before they take effect wherever reasonably possible. The project will explain what is changing, why it is changing, which tokens or holders are affected, and when the change applies.

Nothing on this page authorises the creator to override a published commitment unilaterally. Any amendment must follow the amendment procedure published with the final tokenomics and remain subject to applicable law.
