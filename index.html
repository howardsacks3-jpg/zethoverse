The Vision

ZETHOVERSE is an independent early-stage digital project inspired by the relationship between the very small and the seemingly infinite.

Its creative identity explores movement, energy, evolution and interconnectedness. These imaginative themes provide a starting point for considering what a connected digital ecosystem might become.
