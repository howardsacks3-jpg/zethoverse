FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A straightforward guide to the project, its development and what has not yet been announced.

The project

What is ZETHOVERSE?

ZETHOVERSE is an independent digital project inspired by movement, evolution, interconnectedness and extraordinary possibilities. It explores what a connected digital universe could become. It is being developed in stages.

What does the name mean?

ZETHOVERSE combines the idea of a universe with themes of scale, movement, evolution and connection. This is creative inspiration, not a claim of established scientific theory.

What is the guiding idea?

One tiny universe. Everyone connected. The phrase expresses the project's interest in connection, exploration and the possibility of building meaningful digital experiences.

The coin

What is the coin called?

The final coin name, ticker and artwork are being considered together. They have not yet been approved for public announcement. The project website will publish the official identity when it is ready.

Has the official ZETHOVERSE coin launched?

No. No official ZETHOVERSE coin is currently available to buy. A generic, unbranded test contract was used on Base Sepolia to check the basic deployment process. It is called Test Token (TEST), is not the ZETHOVERSE coin, and is not offered for sale.

How can I identify an official coin?

Use only the official network, contract address and links published on this website at the time of any future release. A similar name, ticker, logo, social profile or token address does not prove that an asset is connected to ZETHOVERSE.

Which blockchain will be used?

The production blockchain has not been finally selected. Technical testing and cost comparisons inform the decision; the official network will be announced with the final project details.

What are the proposed supply, allocation and founder vesting terms?

The current working supply and allocation model, founder vesting schedule, public release safeguards and illustrative launch-price scenarios are published on the ZETHO Token Transparency & Vesting page. These remain working project terms and are not evidence of a minted token or deployed vesting program. The launch price, public distribution mechanism and detailed exceptional-release criteria remain undecided.

Will the coin increase in value or provide returns?

No price performance, financial return, exchange listing or increase in value is promised or implied. Digital assets involve substantial risk, including possible loss of the full amount committed.

Development and contact

What is the roadmap?

The intended sequence is project foundation, coin identity, tokenomics and architecture, technical preparation, a coordinated public reveal, and a launch decision followed by review. See the Roadmap page.

Are partnerships or sponsors confirmed?

No confirmed partnerships, sponsors or commercial arrangements are announced. Any discussions remain exploratory unless formally confirmed and publicly stated.

How can I contact the project?

For general enquiries, ideas, technical or creative collaboration, corporate partnership proposals and sponsorship enquiries, email musket50@hotmail.com.

When will the public reveal happen?

No date has been announced. The intention is to present the completed identity and approved supporting information together, rather than publish unfinished details. Timing depends on design, technical work, costs and appropriate review.
