FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

A straightforward guide to ZETHOVERSE, ZETHO, completed Devnet development and current Mainnet status.

The project

What is ZETHOVERSE?

ZETHOVERSE is an independent digital project inspired by movement, evolution, interconnectedness and extraordinary possibilities. It explores what a connected digital universe could become. It is being developed in stages.

What does the name mean?

ZETHOVERSE combines the idea of a universe with themes of scale, movement, evolution and connection. This is creative inspiration, not a claim of established scientific theory.

What is the guiding idea?

One tiny universe. Everyone connected. The phrase expresses the project's interest in connection, exploration and the possibility of building meaningful digital experiences.

The coin

What is the coin called?

The token is called ZETHO and its ticker is ZETHO. It is the token associated with the ZETHOVERSE project.

Has the official ZETHOVERSE coin launched?

No. ZETHO has been developed and tested on Solana Devnet, but no Mainnet mint has been created. The Devnet mint and programs are test-network components, not a Mainnet token. No public sale is open or funded, and no liquidity pool has been created.

How can I identify an official coin?

Use only the official network, contract address and links published on this website at the time of any future release. A similar name, ticker, logo, social profile or token address does not prove that an asset is connected to ZETHOVERSE.

Which blockchain will be used?

Solana is the selected intended production network. The programs have been built and tested on Solana Devnet. Mainnet deployment has not taken place.

What are the proposed supply, allocation and founder vesting terms?

The current working supply and allocation model, founder vesting schedule, public release safeguards and illustrative launch-price scenarios are published on the ZETHO Token Transparency & Vesting page. The selected framework is a planned 5 billion ZETHO supply with 9 decimals. Founder allocation is 1.5 billion ZETHO, divided into 1.35 billion ordinary vesting and a separately restricted 150 million exceptional reserve. The ordinary schedule has a 12-month cliff within a 36-month total vesting period, followed by monthly releases over the remaining 24 months. The exceptional reserve is disabled and unavailable unless separate protections and review are established. These terms do not mean the Mainnet token or vesting vault has been created.

Will the coin increase in value or provide returns?

No price performance, financial return, exchange listing or increase in value is promised or implied. Digital assets involve substantial risk, including possible loss of the full amount committed.
