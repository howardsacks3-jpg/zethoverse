01 · Overview

ZETHOVERSE is an independent digital project built around an imaginative proposition: even the smallest point can belong to something vast, connected and full of possibility.

The project brings together a cosmic-inspired identity and an ambition to explore digital experiences shaped by participation, creativity and technology. Its identity and product design are being developed in stages.

This document explains the project's current direction. It is not a final technical specification, investment recommendation, promise of functionality or offer to sell a digital asset.

02 · The vision

The ZETHOVERSE vision is inspired by the relationship between the very small and the seemingly infinite. It imagines a digital universe in which people, ideas and future experiences may connect and evolve.

One tiny universe. Everyone connected.

The ambition is not to claim that a new scientific theory has been established. Cosmological and very-small-scale themes are used as creative inspiration for exploring digital connection and possibility.

03 · Inspiration and identity

The project's visual direction draws on deep space, scale, energy, movement, evolution and interconnectedness. A dark field, gold highlights and subtle blue light express the intended atmosphere.

The final coin artwork and visual identity will be presented together when approved. This document does not disclose unfinished artwork or unapproved brand details.

07 · Token supply, allocation and founder safeguards

The selected project framework is a planned total supply of 5 billion ZETHO, with 9 decimal places, on Solana. The allocation below describes the intended distribution of that supply; it does not mean tokens have been minted on Mainnet or that funds have been committed.

Planned allocation

  • Founder — 30%: 1.5 billion ZETHO.
  • Public allocation — 50%: 2.5 billion ZETHO.
  • Liquidity allocation — 5%: 250 million ZETHO.
  • Marketing and community — 5%: 250 million ZETHO.
  • Development reserve — 10%: 500 million ZETHO.

Founder allocation

The founder allocation is divided into an ordinary vesting portion of 1.35 billion ZETHO and a separately restricted exceptional reserve capped at 150 million ZETHO.

The selected schedule has a 12-month cliff within a 36-month total vesting period, followed by monthly releases over the remaining 24 months. No ordinary founder tokens are scheduled for release during the cliff. The exceptional reserve is disabled and is not available for release unless separate independent protections and review are established and the relevant rules are approved and disclosed.

The public allocation is reserved for a future distribution model. This document does not announce an open sale, guarantee availability, or create an entitlement to acquire tokens. Any future distribution terms will be published separately before any offer or transaction is made.

Read the ZETHO Token Transparency & Vesting page for the allocation framework and current implementation status.

These are the project's selected planning parameters. They are not evidence of a Mainnet mint, completed allocation transfers, live vesting vault, public sale or liquidity pool. Mainnet implementation and any future distribution remain subject to separate approval.

08 · Development status

The ZETHO Solana programs have been built and tested on Devnet. The public-sale program and founder-vesting program were exercised in controlled Devnet tests. The deployment console was used to initialise the Devnet sale configuration.

The current Devnet release candidate is frozen. The fresh Anchor build reproduced the downloaded Devnet program binaries byte-for-byte in the recorded build environment. This is a build-reproducibility result, not a formal independent security audit or a guarantee that software is free of vulnerabilities.

Mainnet status: The ZETHO Mainnet mint and an initial Raydium CPMM liquidity pool exist. This litepaper does not certify the status of separate vesting or sale programs, does not promise liquidity or market value, and does not authorise any further expenditure, token movement or program change.

No independent professional security audit has been commissioned. The project's internal development and testing history should not be described as an independent audit.
