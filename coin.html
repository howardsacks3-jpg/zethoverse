01 · The idea behind ZETHO

Three centres. One connected universe.

The three luminous centres in the ZETHO emblem are more than decoration. They represent distinct points joined through a wider network: separate centres, connected by shared pathways, forming one greater pattern.

That image draws on the cosmic web observed across the universe. Galaxies gather along enormous filaments of matter, with vast cosmic voids between them. The web is not a literal golden mesh, but a real large-scale structure revealed through astronomical observation and mapping. It gives ZETHOVERSE its central visual metaphor: connection can emerge across immense distances.

ZETHO is intended as a simple, community-driven meme-coin concept with a distinctive story and identity. The coin is one possible expression of the wider ZETHOVERSE idea; it does not itself prove or implement the scientific concepts that inspire the project.

02 · From the very small to the vast

A journey through the boundaries of what we know.

The Planck boundary

Modern physics points to the Planck length — approximately 1.616 × 10−35 metres — as a scale where quantum effects of gravity are expected to become important. It is a fundamental reference in theoretical physics, not a confirmed smallest pixel of space and not proof that reality ends there.

The Zeto idea

ZETHOVERSE introduces LZeto = 1 × 10−21 metres as a project-defined reference at the zeptometre scale. It is approximately 100 billion times smaller than a typical atom, and around 60 trillion times larger than the Planck length. This is a creative reference for exploring scale, not a newly discovered physical length, recognised scientific unit or established theory.

Dark matter and dark energy

Cosmology also reveals profound unknowns on the largest scales. Dark matter is inferred from its gravitational effects and helps explain how galaxies and cosmic structures form. Dark energy is the name given to whatever is driving the observed accelerated expansion of the universe. They are different questions: dark matter helps structure the cosmic web, while dark energy is associated with the expansion of space.

Black holes, white holes and the holographic idea

Black holes challenge our understanding of gravity, information and spacetime. White holes appear in some mathematical solutions but have not been observed. Speculative ideas ask whether a universe could emerge from a black-hole-like process, or whether information describing a region could be encoded on a lower-dimensional boundary. The holographic principle has important foundations in theoretical physics, especially black-hole entropy and gauge/gravity dualities, but it does not establish that we live inside a black hole.

Could the smallest parts of reality hold clues to how the whole universe is connected?

These are inspirations and open questions, not claims that ZETHOVERSE has solved cosmology. The project aims to keep established findings, active scientific research and imaginative speculation clearly distinguished.

05 · Founder lock, vesting and exceptional release

Transparent rules. No surprise supply.

The current founder-release proposal begins with a six-month lock-up from the public launch date. After that, the founder allocation would vest gradually in monthly portions over the following 36 months. Any portion not released remains locked; deferred amounts would not accumulate into a sudden catch-up release. The schedule and actual movements should be publicly verifiable on-chain.

The exceptional market-strength provision

A sharp price rise or headline peak must never be treated as proof of sustainable demand. Under a narrowly defined, pre-published provision, a limited part of the founder allocation could become eligible for early release only when several market-health conditions are met together:

  • A specified price threshold is reached and sustained over an observation period.
  • Trading volume remains meaningful over time, rather than appearing as a brief spike.
  • Market liquidity is sufficient and independently observable.
  • Buying interest and holder distribution are not dominated by a small number of wallets or related parties.
  • Available evidence does not indicate artificial inflation or market manipulation.

Illustrative early-release cap: 5–10% of the founder's 1.5 billion allocation, equivalent to 75–150 million tokens in total. This would be a cumulative maximum, not an automatic release and not a fresh allowance at every price peak. The final amount and qualifying tests are not approved.

If a qualifying event occurs, only the capped amount could become eligible for early release. The rest of the founder allocation remains under its normal vesting schedule. A market crash would not force the founder to sell or trigger an additional release; a later recovery would not automatically cancel the remaining allocation.

Unlocking is not selling. An unlocked token is transferable, but that does not guarantee it can be sold at the quoted price. Actual proceeds depend on buyers, available liquidity, price impact and slippage. The provision is intended to balance a limited founder opportunity during exceptional demand with predictable supply rules for the community.

Before any launch, the precise thresholds, observation windows, data sources, enforcement method and cumulative cap must be published. Any oracle, external data feed, authorised signer or governance process used to enforce market conditions would require careful security design and independent review. The rules must not be changed retrospectively to benefit insiders.

06 · Trust and risk

Designed for transparency, never a promise of safety.

Visible supply

Final supply, allocations, vesting wallets and contract permissions should be disclosed so that holders can verify them.

Liquidity clarity

Any liquidity arrangements, lock duration, control rights and withdrawal conditions should be disclosed before launch. A token allocation alone does not create liquidity.

Contract review

Production code, minting powers, ownership controls and release mechanisms should be tested and independently reviewed before deployment.

Honest risk disclosure

Crypto assets are highly volatile. A token can lose most or all of its value, liquidity can disappear, and a recovery is never guaranteed.

No tokenomics can make a coin rug-proof, guarantee demand, prevent every form of manipulation or ensure that a price will recover after a crash. The purpose of transparent rules is to reduce avoidable risks and make the project's decisions easier to inspect.
